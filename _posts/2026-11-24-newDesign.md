title: The website has a new design
date: 2026-11-24
---

The HotCarbon website has a new design. It is lighter, works better on phones and keeps every edition in one place.

- Each edition has its own pages with the call for papers, the venue and the program, for example [HotCarbon 2026]({{ '/2026/' | relative_url }}). The calls for papers of [2024]({{ '/2024/cfp/' | relative_url }}) and [2025]({{ '/2025/cfp/' | relative_url }}) are back online as well.
- All papers, slides and talk recordings of [past editions]({{ '/past/' | relative_url }}) stay available.
- The site loads no web fonts, no JavaScript and no trackers. A page now weighs about 30 kB instead of about 450 kB.
